TO THE GIRL WHO BECAME MY FAVORITE PART OF THE UNIVERSE

My Krushna,

If someone had told me near the end of July 2024 that a random game would introduce me to someone who would become this important to me, I probably wouldn't have believed them.

But then there was you. First, just a conversation in August. Then more conversations. More laughs. More late nights. More little moments that slowly started meaning more than they probably should have.

And then came 19 September 2024.

Three simple words.
I love you.
Somehow, those three words became one of the most beautiful parts of my life.

Then came 22 June 2025 — our first call. After all those messages, finally hearing your voice made the distance feel just a little smaller.

And there's something special about our story that I don't want to forget.

We still haven't met. We still don't have that first photo together. We haven't lived those moments that one day we'll probably look back on and smile about.

But that doesn't mean our story is incomplete.

It simply means some of my favorite memories with you haven't happened yet.

Somewhere ahead of us is that first meeting. The first time we'll finally stand in front of each other instead of behind a screen. The first photo. The first hug. The first ordinary day that somehow won't feel ordinary at all.

And until that day comes, I want you to know one thing.

I would choose this story again.

I would choose every conversation, every call, every laugh, every late-night moment, and every kilometre between us.

Because somehow, through all that distance, I found you.

Always yours, Aryan ❤️
